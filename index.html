CLAUSE 2 — most people misfile what this is before they try it.

Most advice about your future asks you to feel differently. FUTUREYOU asks you to act differently — by finding the specific negotiation, in the specific relationship or room, that is currently costing you the most.

A negotiation isn't just a salary conversation. It's any moment where what you want and what's currently being offered don't match — a promotion, a boundary, a partnership, an exit, a second chance. Most people never name that moment. They absorb the gap instead.

We start by finding that moment with you, then prepare you to walk into it and change the outcome.

CLAUSE 3 — six stages, in order. You don't skip to the ask.
01

Understand

We map the situation as it actually is — the people, the power, the timeline, the constraints you haven't said out loud yet.

02

Question

We challenge the assumptions holding the situation in place. Most of what feels fixed is a belief, not a fact.

03

Reframe

We rebuild the situation around what's actually true — often revealing more room to negotiate than you thought existed.

04

Negotiate

We identify your leverage, your counterpart's real incentives, and the terms worth asking for.

05

Act

We prepare the actual conversation — language, sequencing, fallback positions — so you walk in ready.

06

Become

The outcome compounds. Each negotiation you win changes what's negotiable next.

CLAUSE 4 — THE LEVERAGE PRINCIPLE

Most people are negotiating the wrong things — small, low-stakes asks that feel safe but change little. A small number of possible negotiations account for most of the difference between the future you're heading toward and the one you actually want.

Our first job is finding which negotiation, in your specific life, is carrying that weight. Not every conversation deserves this kind of preparation. The right one does.
